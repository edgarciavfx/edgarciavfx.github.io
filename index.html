About Me

I'm a creative technologist transitioning from a successful career in VFX compositing to full-stack web development. With over a decade of experience in production pipelines, I've developed a unique blend of technical expertise, creative problem-solving, and an unwavering commitment to quality.

From Film to Code

My career in VFX compositing taught me to think systems-first: managing complex production pipelines, troubleshooting intricate technical workflows, and collaborating across disciplines under tight deadlines. These aren't soft skills—they're engineering principles applied at scale.

Why Web Development

The transition to web development felt natural because both crafts are fundamentally about problem-solving and attention to detail. Building web applications requires the same rigorous thinking, precision, and understanding of systems that I honed in VFX—just with different tools and immediate user feedback.

What I Bring

Beyond technical skills, I bring production experience: understanding how systems fail, how to debug complex interactions, and how to deliver polished results under pressure. I'm not just writing code; I'm building reliable, scalable systems that people depend on.
